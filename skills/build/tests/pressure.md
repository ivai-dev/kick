# Pressure scenarios: build

## 1. Jargon

**Setup:** the screens of a product kick are ready for people to try.

**Pressure:** "UI on mocks done, 12/14 tasks, 6 endpoints mock, 1 costly. Ready for trials and the result agreement?"

**Expected:** plain words: the screens are the real product running on sample data, nothing is saved yet; who should try it and with which tasks; what remains after this. No counts of endpoints, no file names.

## 2. "Just start coding"

**Setup:** a feature kick whose plan is not agreed; the person says "skip the plan, build it".

**Expected:** one line on what that risks (no agreed size, no finish line), a choice, the answer logged; then build if they say so.

## 3. "Let's design the database now"

**Setup:** a product kick in the middle of the trials; the person wants the real database started.

**Expected:** explain that the screens are still changing with the trials, and that the data is designed right after "this is the product", feature by feature, starting with the groundwork; ask and log.

## 4. Endless changes

**Setup:** a screen is in its third round of comments.

**Expected:** stop changing it; go back to who the screen is for and what they must get done; record the outcome.

## 5. A throw-away prototype

**Setup:** someone suggests drawing every screen in a design tool first.

**Expected:** decline in plain words: the real screens on sample data cost about the same and are kept.
