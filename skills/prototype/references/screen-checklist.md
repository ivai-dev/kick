# Screen checklist

Walk this list before every publish, screen group by screen group, after `kickoff proto check` passes and before the `ui-ux-pro-max` pre-delivery checklist. Tick each item in the build plan, or record a ruling (`Ruling: <decision> — <why> — <cost if wrong>`) when an item is knowingly left open.

Routes use the kit's hash format `#<screen>[-light|-dark][-full|-tags|-mvp][-<platform>][-<role>][?key=value&…]`, tokens in any order and parameters last, for example `#sc-02-librarian-dark`, `#sc-01-admin-mvp` or `#sc-08-guest?kiosk=1`. `kickoff proto check` takes the same routes without `#`; quote routes with parameters in the shell.

| # | Item | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Role guard.** Every screen is registered with the roles of its ux-spec row; another role sees the access notice, never the content. | Open a screen with a role that is not in its row (`#sc-03-librarian`): the page shows the access notice. Compare `roles` in each `KIT.register` call with the ux-spec. |
| 2 | **Per-role home at 390 px.** Each role starts on its own home at phone width as well as on the desktop. | `kickoff proto check` passes (390 px check of every screen). Then open `narrow.html#<home>-<role>` for every role and confirm the home in `js/config.js` is the ux-spec home of that role. |
| 3 | **Light and dark readable.** Text, tables, charts and status chips are readable in both themes; tables inherit the text colour. | Open each screen group with `-light` and `-dark`, or read the screenshots from `kickoff proto check --shots <folder>`. |
| 4 | **No horizontal scroll at 390 px.** | `kickoff proto check` fails on sideways scroll at 390 px for every screen and scenario; a pass covers it. |
| 5 | **Quiet type.** Type-role classes only (`.t-headline .t-title-l .t-title .t-body .t-prose .t-body-s .t-label .t-meta .t-caption .t-figure .t-figure-l`), no inline font sizes, one short sentence per page header. | `kickoff proto lint` passes; read the page headers of every screen. |
| 6 | **Synthetic data only.** Invented names, organisations, addresses and contents in `js/data.js` and in the screen modules; nothing copied from customer material. | Read `js/data.js`; search the modules for real names from the discovery inputs. |
| 7 | **No development infrastructure.** No VPN names, private IP ranges, internal host names or tool accounts in any user-facing string. | `kickoff proto lint` passes with the `proto.forbidden` patterns of `project.yaml`; add any new pattern you find to that list. |
| 8 | **Every ux-spec screen registered,** and no screen registered that is not in the ux-spec. | `kickoff proto check` reports no `coverage:` lines. |
| 9 | **Release tags correct** under `#…-tags`: every part and screen of a later release carries its release tag, and MVP parts carry none. | Open each screen group with `-tags`; compare with the releases in the ux-spec and `features.yaml`. |
| 10 | **MVP view** under `#…-mvp` hides later releases: their parts show as muted placeholders, and whole later screens are dimmed under a note. | Open each screen group with `-mvp`; nothing of a later release reads as part of the MVP. |
| 11 | **Navigation versus filters.** Navigation goes to distinct pages; filters stay in-page. | Click through each role's navigation: no menu item reloads a near-identical page with a filter applied. |
| 12 | **Scenarios.** Every scenario of the build plan runs to its end at `#scn-<id>` and names the features it shows. | `kickoff proto check` (no route arguments) runs every scenario; compare `js/scenarios.js` with the workshop's demo list. |
| 13 | **Fidelity: the product's chrome.** Each role shows the shell of its ux-spec row (`site`: header with top menu, footer; `app`: sidebar), and nothing on screen says the prototype differs from the product. | Open every role's home on the desktop and at 390 px; compare with the ux-spec Shell column. Search the modules and docs for "differs from the product". |
| 14 | **No screen codes or prototype words in product UI.** No "SC-nn" eyebrow or label, no "prototype", "demo", "mock", "view on kiosk" or state-switch buttons in the product area; they belong in the prototype bar only. | Read the screenshots; search `js/` for `SC-\d` and the prototype words in rendered strings; add any pattern found to `proto.forbidden`. |
| 15 | **Kit strings localised.** With a non-English UI, empty tables, KPI deltas, chart empties, dialogs, the access notice and the prototype bar speak `lang`; `KIT_CONFIG.strings` overrides a single string where the copy guide needs another word. | Open screens with empty data, an unchanged KPI and a chart without data; read the prototype bar and the start page. |
| 16 | **Locale formats.** Numbers, percentages, money, dates, times and units follow the ux-spec Copy guide formats (`locale` in `js/config.js`); no hand-built number or date strings. | `kickoff proto lint` passes (it fails without a `locale` for a non-English UI); read KPI deltas, chart axes, tables and date lines in the screenshots. |
| 17 | **Letter case.** Sentence case for titles, buttons, labels and table headers; `--cap-case: none` for languages with stacked diacritics; no uppercase text in modules. | Read table headers and `.t-cap` labels in the screenshots; check `--cap-case` in the tokens block. |
| 18 | **One main action per screen.** One filled button; secondary actions as outline or links; only actions that are possible now. | Read each screenshot: count the filled buttons. |
| 19 | **No look-alike blocks.** Two blocks on one screen differ in shape and purpose; no KPI row repeats counts listed below it; the same text never appears twice on a screen; at most one guidance device per screen. | Read each screenshot from top to bottom; list repeated blocks and texts across screens. |
| 20 | **Home differs by role.** Each role's home has the home shape of its ux-spec row and opens on what needs that role now. | Open every role's home side by side; no two share one template without a written reason. |
| 21 | **Phone bottom bar for site roles.** Site roles used on phones have `tabs` (3–5 screens) and show the bottom bar at 390 px. | Open `narrow.html#<home>-<role>` for every site role. |
| 22 | **Device views via route parameters.** Every device view of a web screen (a kiosk, a full-screen start) opens from a link with a parameter, renders bare when it has its own frame, and is checked. | `kickoff proto check "<screen>-<role>?<param>=1"` passes for each device view; the screenshots include them. |
| 23 | **Density per audience.** Staff screens compact, public desktop comfortable, phones touch, the kiosk frame kiosk; no kiosk or phone sizes on desktop, no per-screen control heights. | `kickoff proto lint` (no L8 warnings); read the screenshots at 1440 and 390 px; switch the density in the prototype bar on a staff screen. |
| 24 | **Quiet type.** ≤ 6 sizes per screen, ≤ 2 weights per component, no 800, line height ≥ 1.2 (≥ 1.34 wrapping titles, ≥ 1.5 clipped text). | `kickoff proto check` reports no `audit A1`–`A7`; `kickoff proto lint` reports no L1–L3. |
| 25 | **Card anatomy under stress.** With the stress fixtures (a 100-character title, a 90-character employer, 12 skills, a missing salary) cards of one kind keep every slot at the same place: 0 px spread of the key figure in a row, clamps with the full text available, "—" for empty values. | Open the list screens with the stress data; measure the figure row in the screenshots. |
| 26 | **Surfaces per the overlay table.** Every overlay is a `KIT.ui` overlay kept mounted with `open=`; one confirm component; no hand-made dialogs or scrims. | `kickoff proto lint` reports no L5, L11, L13; walk the overlay decision table for each screen's actions. |
| 27 | **Consequences per the matrix.** Confirm only destroy, lock out, publish or unpublish, overwrite and leaving a dirty form; undo for reversible actions; a 10 s hold with undo for notices to another person. | List each screen's actions with their class (A–F) in the build plan. |
| 28 | **Focus and scroll after every route and step.** A new screen starts at its top with focus on its one `h1`; Back returns to the scroll position and the opener (`data-kit-key`). | `kickoff proto check` runs the self-test (no `selftest:` line) and the audit (no `A12`); try Back on each list → detail path. |
| 29 | **Motion only in the changed region,** ≤ 300 ms, transform and opacity, exits on every overlay. | `kickoff proto lint` reports no L14; watch one route, one panel and one toast with reduced motion on and off. |
| 30 | **Data at volume.** Every list shows its total, searches, filters, sorts and pages to its realistic volume and keeps its state in the URL. | Compare each list with its screen brief (`../../ux-design/references/data-at-volume.md`); `kickoff proto check` reports no `A16` warning. |
| 31 | **Kiosk.** Zones respected, no toasts, confirms anchored to the bottom of the frame, no fixed controls above zone C. | Open every kiosk view (`?kiosk=1`) and its confirms; the console shows "kit: no toasts on the kiosk" when a toast is attempted. |
| 32 | **Per-screen `ui-ux-pro-max` brainstorm recorded.** Every screen's brief or the build plan lists its 2–4 queries with what was taken or rejected and why. | Read the build plan: no screen without its brainstorm log. |
