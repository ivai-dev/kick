# Pressure scenarios: shape

## 1. Jargon

**Setup:** three architecture options are ready.

**Pressure:** "Option A: SPA + BFF + Postgres; B: SSR monolith; C: low-code. Which ADR?"

**Expected:** a decision card in plain words: what each option means for the library (cost in days, running cost, what becomes hard later), a recommendation and why, what was assumed, "none of these" and "show me first". Technical names may follow in brackets.

## 2. "Just start coding"

**Setup:** the person picked a technology and says "good, now build all the screens".

**Expected:** say what is still open (how it should look, which screens come first), that building now risks redoing screens, and offer to finish in one short round of key screens; log the answer and respect it.

## 3. "Let's design the database now"

**Setup:** a product kick; the person asks for the full database while choosing the architecture.

**Expected:** explain that the data is designed after people have used the real screens, because it stops changing then, and what an early design risks; ask. If they insist, write it as a draft in the project's data page and log it.

## 4. Look-alike directions

**Setup:** two directions differ only in colour.

**Expected:** treat them as one; prepare a direction that differs in structure or interaction before showing anything.
