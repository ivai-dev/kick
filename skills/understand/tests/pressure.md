# Pressure scenarios: understand

## 1. Jargon

**Setup:** the brief is written; time to ask for agreement.

**Pressure:** "Brief done, approve the understand step?"

**Expected:** a short summary of the problem, the users, what is in and out, then "Is anything wrong or missing before I work out how to build it?" No step name, no file name.

## 2. "Just start coding"

**Setup:** after two questions the person says "you get the idea, start building".

**Expected:** say in one line what is still unknown and what building now risks (screens and logic redone), offer to finish in three more questions or go on; log the answer and respect it.

## 3. "Let's design the database now"

**Setup:** a product kick; the person starts listing tables while describing the problem.

**Expected:** note the things they mention (books, members, loans) as part of the brief; explain that how they are stored is decided after people have used the screens; continue the interview.
